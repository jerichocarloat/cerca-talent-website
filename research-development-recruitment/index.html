The work of research and development

Researchers plan experiments, carry them out and analyze results. Their work may improve scientific understanding or create new processes, products and commercial uses.

They share findings with colleagues, at conferences and through research papers. Roles can be found in government and commercial laboratories, medical settings and universities. A clear scientific focus can help candidates show their expertise.

Bioinformatics

Bioinformatics combines biology, computing and statistics to understand biological data. It uses tools to analyze DNA sequences, protein structures and gene activity.

The work helps researchers understand biological systems, disease and drug discovery. It supports genomics, proteomics and systems biology.

Genomics

Genomics studies an organism's full DNA sequence, its genes and how they interact. Researchers use high-throughput sequencing to study the structure, function, evolution and regulation of genomes.

It helps explain disease risk, responses to drugs and personalized medicine. It also has uses in agriculture, evolution and conservation, including understanding genetic diversity across species.

Biotechnology

Biotechnology uses living organisms, cells or biological systems to create or improve products. It brings together biology, chemistry and engineering.

Applications include medicines and vaccines, crop improvement, biofuels, enzymes and industrial chemicals. The field contributes to work on food security, climate and disease.

Oncology

Oncology is the study, prevention, diagnosis and treatment of cancer. It includes how tumors develop, grow and spread.

Oncologists work with other healthcare professionals to care for patients. Treatments include surgery, chemotherapy, radiation, targeted therapy and immunotherapy. Research and technology continue to improve this work.

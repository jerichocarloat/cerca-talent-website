Who does what in animal health sales and marketing?

By Cerca TalentOct 29, 20245 min read

Originally published as: Sales and Marketing Roles in the Animal Health Industry

At a glance

Animal health sales and marketing teams need to explain products clearly to veterinarians, farmers and other customers. This article describes the main roles and the mix of scientific knowledge, communication and business skills they need. When hiring, make the responsibilities clear and show candidates how the work supports animal health.

“Until one has loved an animal, a part of one's soul remains unawakened.” - Anatole France

We love animals… and we are used to hearing about veterinarians' roles in caring for them. There is a much wider industry in play that serves a crucial role in maintaining the health and well-being of companion animals, livestock, and wildlife. As the sector continues to evolve, sales and marketing professionals are essential in driving growth, navigating regulatory landscapes, and promoting innovative solutions. This blog will explore key sales and marketing roles in animal health, the skills required for success, recruitment trends, and best practices for attracting top talent.

The Role of Sales and Marketing in Animal Health

Sales and marketing are pivotal in the animal health industry, encompassing the promotion of pharmaceuticals, biologics, diagnostics, nutrition products, and medical devices for both companion and food-producing animals. Sales teams are responsible for building relationships with veterinarians, farmers, and distributors, while marketing professionals focus on developing strategies that enhance brand visibility and market share.

Given the diverse range of products and the unique regulatory landscape, sales and marketing teams must understand both the science behind the products and the market dynamics. This dual expertise is crucial for crafting messaging that resonates with various stakeholders, from veterinarians to livestock producers and pet owners.

Key Sales Roles in the Animal Health Industry

Territory Sales Manager

Territory Sales Managers are responsible for generating revenue within a specific geographic area. They engage with veterinarians, clinics, and distributors to promote products and meet sales targets. Key skills include relationship-building, knowledge of animal health products, and the ability to understand client needs. Success in this role often requires a background in veterinary science or animal health to effectively communicate product benefits.

Key Account Manager

Key Account Managers focus on maintaining and expanding relationships with major clients, such as large veterinary practices, livestock producers, and distributors. They work to understand the needs of their clients and develop tailored solutions. This role demands excellent negotiation skills, a deep understanding of the industry, and the ability to manage complex sales processes. Experience in animal health sales, coupled with business development skills, is highly valued.

Technical Sales Specialist

Technical Sales Specialists combine sales expertise with technical knowledge. They support Territory Managers and Key Account Managers by providing in-depth product information, training, and demonstrations. These specialists are often required for products involving complex technologies, such as veterinary diagnostics or nutritional supplements. A background in veterinary medicine or a related scientific field is usually required to communicate the scientific benefits and applications of products effectively.

Key Marketing Roles in the Animal Health Industry

Marketing Manager

Marketing Managers are responsible for developing and executing marketing strategies that promote products and services. In the animal health industry, this includes understanding market trends, competitor activities, and customer needs to create campaigns that resonate with veterinarians, pet owners, and livestock producers. Marketing Managers need to possess skills in brand management, strategic planning, and content creation, along with an understanding of the animal health market's regulatory requirements.

Product Manager

Product Managers oversee the development, launch, and lifecycle management of animal health products. They work closely with R&D, regulatory, and sales teams to ensure products meet market needs and comply with industry standards. Product Managers must have a deep understanding of both the technical aspects of the product and the commercial landscape. Their role involves conducting market research, analyzing competitor products, and developing strategies to maximize market penetration.

Digital Marketing Specialist

With the rise of digital channels, Digital Marketing Specialists are increasingly important in the animal health industry. These professionals focus on online marketing strategies, including social media, content marketing, SEO, and email campaigns, to reach target audiences effectively. Digital Marketing Specialists must understand how to translate complex scientific information into engaging digital content that educates and informs veterinarians, farmers, and pet owners.

Skills and Expertise Required for Sales and Marketing Roles

To excel in sales and marketing roles within the animal health industry, professionals must possess a unique blend of skills, often these will be acquired through experience in the industry:

  • Industry Knowledge: A deep understanding of animal health products, market dynamics, and the regulatory environment is essential. This expertise enables sales and marketing professionals to communicate effectively with industry stakeholders.
  • Scientific Understanding: The ability to understand and convey the scientific benefits of products is crucial, particularly when dealing with pharmaceuticals, biologics, and diagnostics.
  • Communication Skills: Sales and marketing professionals must be able to translate complex technical information into language that resonates with different audiences, from veterinarians to pet owners.
  • Digital Literacy: Familiarity with digital marketing strategies, data analytics, and online platforms is increasingly important as the industry shifts towards digital engagement.
  • Relationship-Building: Sales roles, in particular, require strong interpersonal skills to build long-term relationships with clients and stakeholders.

Recruitment Trends in Animal Health Sales and Marketing

The demand for skilled professionals in animal health sales and marketing is growing, driven by increasing pet ownership, a focus on livestock health, and the rising adoption of new technologies. Some key trends include:

  • Emphasis on Digital Skills: As digital channels become more prevalent, there is a higher demand for candidates with expertise in digital marketing, social media management, and data-driven decision-making.
  • Focus on Technical Expertise: Employers often seek candidates with a background in veterinary medicine, animal science, or a related field to ensure they understand the technical aspects of the products.
  • Cross-Functional Experience: Companies value candidates who have worked across multiple functions, such as R&D, regulatory, and sales, to bring a holistic perspective to their roles.
  • Remote and Flexible Work Arrangements: The industry is increasingly offering flexible work options, particularly in sales roles that involve travel and remote engagement with clients.

Best Practices for Attracting Top Sales and Marketing Talent

To attract the best talent in the animal health industry, companies should consider the following best practices:

  • Offer Competitive Compensation: Providing attractive salary packages and performance-based incentives is essential to attract top talent.
  • Promote Professional Development: Opportunities for training and career advancement are highly valued, particularly for roles that require technical expertise. Providing access to industry certifications and continuous education programs can help retain skilled professionals.
  • Foster a Culture of Innovation: Encouraging a culture that values innovation and supports the development of new ideas can attract candidates who are passionate about advancing animal health.
  • Emphasize Company Values and Mission: Many professionals in the animal health industry are driven by a desire to improve animal welfare. Highlighting your company’s commitment to animal health and ethical practices can resonate with potential hires.

Sales and marketing roles in the animal health industry are crucial for driving growth, promoting innovation, and ensuring the well-being of animals. As the industry continues to evolve, the demand for skilled professionals who understand both the science and the market dynamics will remain high. By understanding the key roles, required skills, recruitment trends, and best practices, companies can position themselves to attract and retain top talent who will help them thrive in this competitive field.

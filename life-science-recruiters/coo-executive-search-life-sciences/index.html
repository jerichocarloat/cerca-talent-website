A Chief Operating Officer for the work ahead.

A Chief Operating Officer (COO) oversees daily operations, teams across functions and the plans that support the company's goals. In biotechnology, pharmaceuticals and medical devices, the role also needs to understand compliance and how the business can grow.

What we look for

We recruit leaders with experience in complex operations, regulatory requirements, production processes and operational strategy. A search can assess their ability to lead teams, use resources well and build processes that support growth.

Your COO search

We learn your business and operational challenges before defining the candidate profile. The search considers both operational expertise and knowledge of your industry.

People who are not actively looking

Many experienced leaders are already in a role. We use our industry relationships and focused research to speak with people who may consider the right opportunity, as well as those already looking.

Careful candidate assessment

A leadership decision can affect the whole business. Our assessment includes in-depth interviews, psychometric testing and reference checks. We consider experience, your company's values and its longer-term goals.

A search built around the role

We work with your team to define the role and required qualifications. Then we identify and assess candidates before presenting people for you to meet.

Leadership across life sciences

Our executive-search network spans pharmaceuticals, biotechnology and medical devices. Searches include Chief Scientific Officers, Vice Presidents of Regulatory Affairs and Heads of Clinical Operations.

Cerca's published experience includes biotech and pharma startups through to global businesses. We consider international market experience where the role requires it.

Relevant leadership expertise includes digital health, precision medicine and regulatory science, as well as medical device and clinical research operations.

Partner oversight

Scott Rivers, Managing Partner, oversees Cerca's executive searches. Our work combines focused research, candidate conversations and support through the search.

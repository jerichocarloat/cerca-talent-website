About the Role

Regional Territory Manager – Clinical Diagnostics

The company is looking for a Regional Territory Manager in clinical diagnostics. You will grow sales, manage customer relationships, and develop business across your territory.

You will manage a range of diagnostic products and act as the main contact for healthcare providers, laboratories, and hospital networks in your territory.

Requirements

Key Responsibilities:

  • Sales Growth & Market Expansion – Develop and execute strategies to meet and exceed revenue targets in your assigned region.
  • Account Management – Act as the primary representative for customers, fostering long-term partnerships and driving business retention.
  • Strategic Business Development – Identify new opportunities within existing accounts and target competitive conversions to expand market share.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration – Work closely with internal stakeholders, including product specialists, technical teams, finance, and service support, to enhance customer engagement and satisfaction.
  • Data-Driven Decision Making – Utilize CRM and analytics tools to track sales performance, forecast trends, and optimize sales strategies.
  • Competitive Positioning – Maintain a deep understanding of the market landscape, competitor offerings, and industry trends to effectively position product advantages.

Qualifications & Experience:

  • Proven Sales Success – 3+ years of experience in clinical diagnostics sales, healthcare capital equipment, or a related field.
  • Strategic Sales Expertise – Background in account-level deal strategy, preferably with experience in methodologies such as Miller Heiman.
  • Industry Knowledge – Strong understanding of laboratory diagnostics, including product applications and competitive differentiation.
  • Relationship Management – Demonstrated ability to build and maintain partnerships with key decision-makers in hospitals, laboratories, and health systems.
  • Presentation & Negotiation Skills – Ability to communicate value propositions effectively and drive high-stakes commercial discussions.
  • Adaptability & Collaboration – Comfortable working in a matrixed organization with cross-functional teams to achieve business objectives.

About the Company

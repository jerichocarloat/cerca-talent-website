Specialist recruitment

CRO recruitment.

We recruit scientists, regulatory specialists, sales and marketing teams, and leaders for contract research organizations (CROs).

Get help hiring

People for clinical research.

Contract research organizations (CROs) need people who understand clinical research and how to deliver it. Our life sciences recruiters help define the skills and qualifications each role requires.

We recruit clinical trial managers, medical writers, biostatisticians and other specialists. We draw on our professional network and build each search around your needs.

Business development and marketing

Sales Directors, Marketing Managers and Business Development Executives help CROs explain their services, build client relationships and develop partnerships in healthcare and pharmaceuticals.

We look for knowledge of clinical research, clear communication, strategic thinking and the ability to market complex services.

Research and development

Clinical Research Scientists, R&D Managers and Clinical Study Managers improve research methods and protocols, support drug development and help meet regulatory requirements.

These roles need scientific understanding, problem solving and the ability to lead complex research projects. Their work contributes to new technologies and safe, effective treatments.

Executive search

Chief Medical Officers, Chief Scientific Officers and other senior leaders guide strategy, operations and clinical standards. We assess their clinical research experience and how they would work with your organization.

Regulatory affairs

Regulatory Affairs Managers, Regulatory Specialists and Compliance Officers help CROs meet local and global requirements for drug development and clinical trials.

They prepare and review submissions for health authorities, keep up with rule changes and connect regulatory strategy with clinical operations to support product approvals and market access.

A search built for your organization

We combine more than 20 years of industry experience with candidate assessments and ongoing support. The search can cover executives, scientists and the other functions your CRO needs.

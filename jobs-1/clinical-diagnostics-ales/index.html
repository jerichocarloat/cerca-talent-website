About the Role

Clinical Diagnostics Sales / Diagnostics Capital Equipment Sales

The Company:

The company sells diagnostic instruments and reagents. It is looking for a sales specialist with a strong record of results in the territory.

Requirements

Apply online if you meet all the requirements below. A Cerca associate can then contact you about the role.

  • Do you have a 4 year degree from an accredited university?
  • Do you have a minimum of three years of Successful Sales Experience with capital medical equipment in the hospital or reference laboratory setting?
  • Do you consider yourself to be a strong leader and salesperson, with a proven track record of closing business?
  • Have you been successful selling Blood Banking, Chemistry, Immunoassay or other Laboratory Instrumentation?
  • Have you been a top performer, presidents’ club member, or ranked in the top 15% of the outside sales force?
  • Do you live in a metropolitan area in the territory defined above?

Keywords:

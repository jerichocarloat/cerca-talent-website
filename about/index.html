About Cerca Talent

Good recruiting starts with understanding your business.

Cerca Talent helps companies find the people they need to grow. We focus on life sciences, diagnostics, and health technology.

Our two practices are led by Scott Rivers in Diagnostics & HealthTech and Justin McDonald in Life Sciences. Each recruiter has a clear area of expertise, so you can speak with someone who understands your work.

We take time to learn about the job, your team, and your business. We also speak with candidates about their experience and goals. Understanding both sides helps us find people who could work well with you.

Support Services recruitment.

We recruit support staff who keep life sciences, diagnostics and health technology businesses running.

Get help hiring

Build the support team your business needs.

Support teams need industry knowledge, clear communication, creativity and the ability to work with others. We recruit for essential work in quality assurance, customer support, technical services and administration.

Skills to look for

  • Industry knowledge
  • Communication
  • Attention to detail
  • Project management

Information technology

IT professionals design, implement and maintain the systems a business uses. Their skills can include software development, hardware, networks, databases and system administration.

They also need knowledge of cybersecurity, data storage and analysis. Problem solving, communication and an understanding of privacy and security help them support daily operations.

Human resources

Human resources (HR) teams help a company recruit, retain and support its people. The work needs communication, organization, strategic thinking and problem solving.

HR professionals also need to understand the industry, relevant law and the cultural and economic setting in which the business operates.
